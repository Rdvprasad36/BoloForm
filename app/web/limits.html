Limits

What Bolo Form does not do, and what we have not proven. Update this page with real failures after each eval run.

Known limits of this build

  • Demo only. The forms are made-up examples. Nothing is submitted anywhere. Use fake data.
  • Telugu wording is a draft. Prompts, number words and command words have not been reviewed by a native speaker.
  • District list needs verification against an official source; district names have changed over time.
  • Number parsing is partial. It covers common spoken forms up to thousands, "X and a half", and "double/triple" digits. Unusual or regional number phrasing can fail; the read-back step exists to catch this.
  • Noise estimate is crude. It compares loud and quiet frames; it is not a calibrated SNR measurement.
  • Phone-line mode is a simulation (band-limit, 8 kHz, mu-law), not a real telephone network.
  • Names and village names are free text and are the hardest fields to recognise; they are confirmed by read-back but the spelling may still need correction on the review screen.
  • No live telephony or IVR integration.

Limits of the evaluation

  • Test speech comes from text-to-speech, which is cleaner and more uniform than real people speaking.
  • One synthetic voice cannot represent Telugu dialects (Uttarandhra, Rayalaseema, Coastal, Telangana).
  • The simulated user always notices a wrong read-back and always understands yes/no, which real users will not.
  • Results on the mock provider are simulated and must not be quoted.
  • Small samples give wide confidence intervals.

Privacy

  • No audio is stored. Answers live in memory for the session and are deleted when you finish, press Delete, or after 10 idle minutes.
  • Transcripts are not logged unless the operator enables debug mode.
