# 60-Second Demo Video Script — Bolo Form

**Total Duration**: 60 seconds  
**Main Narrative**: A farmer speaks Telugu; Bolo Form catches a mishearing, confirms critical details, and fills an English enterprise form live via embeddable widget.

---

### Shot 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:08)
- **Visual**: Screen capture of "Demo Agri Co" web page with a daunting 8-field English form.
- **Audio / Voiceover**: "English web forms exclude millions of rural Indian citizens. Meet Bolo Form: voice-first form filling powered by Gnani AI."
- **Caption**: *Bolo Form: Voice-first multilingual form assistant*

---

### Shot 2: Launching the Widget (0:08 – 0:15)
- **Visual**: User clicks "Fill by voice / వాయిస్ ద్వారా నింపండి". The compact Bolo Form overlay opens.
- **Audio / Spoken**: Assistant speaks Telugu: *"నమస్కారం! మీ పేరు ఏమిటి?"* (Hello! What is your name?)
- **User speaks**: *"నా పేరు రాము"* (My name is Ramu)
- **Caption**: *Spoken Telugu prompt (Gnani Timbre TTS) → Telugu voice capture*

---

### Shot 3: Caught Mishearing & Correction (0:15 – 0:28)
- **Visual**: Question asks for Age. User says *"ఇరవై ఆరు"* (26). Assistant mis-hears or simulates: *"ఇరవై ఐదు. సరైనదేనా?"* (25. Is that correct?)
- **User says**: *"కాదు"* (No)
- **Assistant**: *"సరే, మళ్ళీ చెప్పండి."* (Okay, please say it again.)
- **User says**: *"ఇరవై ఆరు"* (26).
- **Assistant confirms**: Committed!
- **Caption**: *Adaptive confirmation catches errors before they commit!*

---

### Shot 4: Critical Field Readback (0:28 – 0:40)
- **Visual**: User speaks phone number *"9876543210"*.
- **Assistant readback**: Digit-by-digit readback: *"9 8 7, 6 5 4, 3 2 1 0. సరైనదేనా?"*
- **User taps or says**: *"అవును"* (Yes).
- **Caption**: *Critical numbers (phone, land acres) verified digit-by-digit*

---

### Shot 5: Live Transfer to Host Form (0:40 – 0:52)
- **Visual**: User clicks "Complete & Transfer". The modal closes. Camera focuses on the English host form.
- **Visual effect**: The fields (Name: Ramu, Age: 26, District: Visakhapatnam, Phone: 9876543210, Crop: Paddy) flash green and populate live!
- **User action**: User clicks the company's own green "Submit to Demo Agri Co" button.
- **Caption**: *Host form populated live via embed postMessage — in English!*

---

### Shot 6: Real Measured Results & Outro (0:52 – 1:00)
- **Visual**: Slide showing real measured numbers:
  - Gnani Prisma STT (p50: 1.6s)
  - Gnani Timbre TTS (p50: 2.2s)
  - 85 automated unit & integration tests passing.
- **Audio / Voiceover**: "Voice-first access for Bharat, verified and accurate. Powered by Gnani AI."
